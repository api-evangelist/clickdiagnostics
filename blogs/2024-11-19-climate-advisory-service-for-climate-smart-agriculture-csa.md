Climate Advisory Service for Climate-Smart Agriculture (CSA) workshop OCTOBER 28, 2024 On October 28, 2024, mPower Social Enterprises Ltd., in collaboration with Friends In Village Development Bangladesh (FIVDB) and Welthungerhilfe (WHH), brought together experts, practitioners, and policymakers to explore pathways to climate resilience in Bangladesh. Our Climate Advisory Service for Climate-Smart Agriculture (CSA) workshop highlighted the role of technology in empowering smallholder farmers with climate adaptation tools and real-time data.
