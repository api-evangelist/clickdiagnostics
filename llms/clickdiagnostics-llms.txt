# mPower Social Enterprises Ltd.

> mPower Social Enterprises Ltd. is a Dhaka, Bangladesh technology-for-development company,
> founded in 2008 by Harvard and MIT graduate students as ClickDiagnostics (winner of the MIT
> $100K competition) and renamed after moving its center of gravity to Bangladesh in 2010. It
> designs, builds and operates information systems for governments, UN agencies, donors and
> international NGOs across health and nutrition, water and sanitation, agriculture, education,
> governance, livelihood, climate and humanitarian response — more than 300 ICT solutions in
> 17 countries in the Global South, on open-source digital public goods (DHIS2, OpenMRS,
> OpenSRP/FHIR Core, Bahmni, Moodle). ISO 9001:2015 certified.

## Status

mPower Social publishes **no public API**, no developer portal, no API reference, and no
machine-readable contract. As of 2026-08-09, probes for `/llms.txt`, `/openapi.json`,
`/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`, `/graphql`, `/.well-known/security.txt`,
`/.well-known/agent-card.json`, `/.well-known/agent.json`, `/.well-known/api-catalog`,
`/.well-known/openid-configuration`, `/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server` and
`/.well-known/ai-plugin.json` all returned **404** on `mpower-social.com`, and
`api.`, `docs.`, `developer.`, `dev.`, `portal.`, `app.` and `data.mpower-social.com` do not
resolve in DNS. One technical subdomain does resolve — `dhis2.mpower-social.com` — but it does
not answer on port 443 from the public internet (TCP timeout), so no DHIS2 Web API surface is
publicly reachable. mPower's work is delivered as bespoke systems inside client institutions,
not as a product with a developer program.

## Platforms mPower implements

These are the open-source platforms mPower customizes and integrates for clients. The APIs
belong to the upstream projects, not to mPower.

- DHIS2 — national health management information systems.
- OpenMRS (including the 3.x / O3 microfrontend stack) — electronic medical records.
- OpenSRP / FHIR Core — offline-first mobile health for frontline workers, on HL7 FHIR.
- Bahmni — hospital and clinic systems.
- Moodle — e-learning platforms.
